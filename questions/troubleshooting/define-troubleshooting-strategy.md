# Define an organization-wide troubleshooting strategy
## Answer guide
- Establish a common incident vocabulary, severity model, ownership boundaries, evidence standards, and escalation path while allowing teams to retain technology-specific runbooks. Measure time to detect, mitigate, recover, and recurring incident classes.
- Fund observability, safe rollout, game days, and on-call training as product capabilities rather than relying on heroic responders. Use a searchable catalog of symptoms, decisions, and verified mitigations with expiry reviews.
- Govern through learning reviews and reliability outcomes, not individual blame or command-count targets. A centralized process that forbids local adaptation will be ignored; a loose process without decision authority permits unsafe parallel changes.
