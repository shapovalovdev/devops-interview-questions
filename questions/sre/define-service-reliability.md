# Define service reliability and the role of an SRE

Define service reliability and the role of an SRE is an SRE interview topic. Explain the mechanism, operational constraints, and failure modes.

## Answer guide

- Reliability is the ability to meet defined user expectations for availability, latency, correctness, and durability; SRE applies software engineering to operate that outcome.
- SRE shares responsibility with product teams for measurable objectives, change safety, monitoring, incident response, capacity, and eliminating repetitive operational work.
- Do not promise universal uptime or make SRE a ticket queue. Define service ownership and the user journey, because provider outages, dependencies, and product choices all affect the outcome.
