# Build a reliability investment roadmap

Build a reliability investment roadmap is an SRE interview topic. Explain the mechanism, operational constraints, and failure modes.

## Answer guide

- Prioritize investments using customer impact, measured risk, SLO gaps, incident history, dependency concentration, and cost—not a generic list of best practices.
- Balance prevention, detection, mitigation, recovery, and operability; assign owners and milestones, and validate progress with drills, SLO trends, and reduced operational burden.
- Do not fund only visible outages or over-index on one technology. Roadmaps must account for organizational ownership, data recovery, and the risk introduced by change itself.
