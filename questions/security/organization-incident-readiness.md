# Build organization-wide incident readiness

How would you make security incident response dependable across product, platform, and legal teams?

## Answer guide

- Establish roles, escalation paths, communications, evidence handling, containment authority, and vendor contacts before an incident; map them to service ownership and on-call coverage.
- Standardize high-value telemetry and run scenario exercises that include technical recovery, executive decisions, customer communication, and third-party dependencies.
- Measure time to detect, contain, recover, and complete corrective actions; turn exercise and incident findings into funded engineering work.
- A runbook nobody can access under outage conditions is ineffective. Over-centralizing every decision delays containment, while uncoordinated teams can destroy evidence or communicate inconsistent facts.
