# Must-know DevOps study collection

This is a deliberately small, cross-theme collection for a focused review, including a two-hour certification-prep pass. It does not create a duplicate Question or replace a certification curriculum.

## Selection criteria

A `must-know` Question must meet every condition:

1. It tests a transferable operational concept rather than product trivia.
2. Its canonical Theme is represented at most twice, so one platform cannot dominate the collection.
3. Its answer guide has structured primary-source verification and a complementary learning resource.
4. Its `## What to learn next` section has exactly five audited, categorized resources in the link-audit manifest.
5. Together, the collection spans Linux/processes, containers, Kubernetes, security, observability/incident response, cloud, distributed systems, and messaging.

The site filter is available at [`index.html#collection=must-know`](../index.html#collection=must-know). Revisit the selection when audited coverage or core platform practices change.
